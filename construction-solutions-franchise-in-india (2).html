3. What is a Construction Solutions Franchise?

What is a Construction Solutions Franchise?

A Construction Solutions Franchise is a business model where a franchisee operates a construction service unit under the franchisor’s brand name and systems. The franchisee manages local projects, clients, and execution, while the franchisor provides branding, technical guidance, standard processes, and business support.


This model reduces the risk of independent operations and helps franchise partners scale their business with structured support.
